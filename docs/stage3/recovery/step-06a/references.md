## Comparative judgment

MPA-TRS is no longer weaker simply because it uses simpler or flatter shapes. Its warm editorial voice, open programme index, concrete field band and independent work relationships are appropriate institutional strengths. It falls short mainly in public copy curation, availability signalling, scan hierarchy and the visual presentation of accumulated history. TinggalJalan's advantage is coordinated purpose, media and task information; its commercial ornament is not the lesson.
