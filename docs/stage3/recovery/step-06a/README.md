# Step 6A - critical whole-site UI/UX audit

16 October 2026. **Audit and proposal only; awaiting owner review.** Owner accepts Step5C-2B. No Step6B, application edit, new photograph, deployment, backend or PR13 merge is authorized by this record.

## Executive judgment

The website is a credible, coherent institutional prototype with a clear visual language, not an interchangeable SaaS template. Its institutional introduction is now literal and legible; programme/edition/activity/work identities remain intelligible; real field photographs and restrained Serif/Sans treatment establish character. The implementation follows the approved Step4 composition closely. Those are strengths to preserve.

It is not yet a fully resolved institutional publication. Its largest weakness is the gap between a sophisticated evidence model and the visitor's reading/discovery experience: much copy describes the review process, while important destinations promise articles but provide only metadata. Several catalogues and related sections need stronger scan hierarchy. Authentic photography has improved the site substantially, but the mobile field pair makes climbing subjects small. Jejak's institutional meaning needs more framing than the existing sequence of activity-derived entries.

No P0 or blocked core journey was identified in this bounded expert walkthrough. Two P1 strong expert findings concern publication expectations and public editorial hierarchy. They are not measured user-study results or permission to release restricted originals. Eight P2 findings/conditions and two P3 hypotheses/options are recorded precisely in the issue register; no aesthetic preference is classified as a blocking defect.

## Five highest-value packages

1. **Truthful knowledge discovery:** availability cues/action wording before click (UX-01) and purposeful related-record grouping (UX-07).
2. **Public editorial voice:** concise activity purpose first; material uncertainty remains visible and full provenance discoverable (UX-02).
3. **Catalogue retrieval:** declared ordering, scannable row anatomy/date hierarchy, clearer existing PD result shortcut, consistent footer destination (UX-03/06/09).
4. **Readable field photography:** evaluate an accessible same-image enlargement or bounded mobile treatment, preserving frame/credit/permission (UX-04).
5. **Institutional continuity:** contextual Jejak framing and a useful compact homepage fallback, without invented history/new media (UX-05/08).

Recommend these targeted changes, preserving Institutional Field Editorial, Source Serif4/Source Sans3, paper/green/blue/gold, A's sequence and all canonical relationships. Do not automatically replace the Kawi hero. It communicates real collective identity and campus context; an outdoor alternative is a testable hypothesis with placement/permission trade-offs, not an established improvement.
