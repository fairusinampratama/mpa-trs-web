# Step 2 decision — institutional publication authority

Owner-confirmed decision · 14 October 2026 · current governing clarification

The project owner confirms: **“I can authorize institutional use of MAPALA Tursina's visual identity and archival materials for the MPA-TRS website”**, subject to applicable copyright, photographer rights, personal privacy and individual consent requirements.

## Operating consequence

- Treat the owner as the designated decision-maker for institutional use within that authority. Do not require an additional institutional representative by default or repeatedly reopen this question.
- Determine ownership or adequate publication permission for each proposed asset. Institutional authority does not replace a photographer's or third party's rights. Seek additional permission only for a concrete asset-specific gap.
- Review recognizable subjects, context and an appropriate privacy/consent basis for the proposed use. Flag sensitive or uncertain cases individually; visibility of a face is not itself proof of either consent or a categorical publication ban.
- Use the three categories: **ready for final owner publication approval**, **requires specific additional permission or verification**, and **unsuitable for public publication**.
- Bring actual private images, identifiers, usage, captions, credits and assessments to the owner. Final approval can cover individual assets or an explicitly enumerated group whose conditions are satisfied.
- This decision is not blanket publication authorization, photo clearance, a theme selection, homepage implementation approval or Step 3 authorization.
