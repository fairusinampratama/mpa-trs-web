## Required later validation and handoff

1. Owner accepts or revises the **visual proposal**.
2. Owner separately accepts or revises the **component specification**; reference names may be adjusted if responsibility is preserved.
3. Resolve canonical emblem and exact **asset-specific publication conditions**. Institutional decision-maker is settled; do not require a second representative by default. Photography remains HOLD until the applicable rights/privacy/credit/use conditions and final approval are resolved.
4. Step 5 begins only on a separate explicit **React implementation instruction**. Visual/component approval is not automatic authorization.
5. Then map approved semantic values to Tailwind4 centrally, reuse matching components, keep UI-lab source shared, and verify every affected page. Preserve programme/canonical record semantics and sparse data. Keep production and backend stages separate.
6. Browser checks must cover actual font render/fallback,200% zoom,320/390/768/1366 layout, no horizontal overflow, keyboard focus/menu/Escape, screen-reader labels/updates, pointer targets, image srcset/loading and console errors. Physical-device limits must be stated.
