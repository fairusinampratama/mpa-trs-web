## Proposed art direction

**Institutional field editorial:** serious, approachable, readable and photographic. Source Serif 4 provides institutional/editorial title character; Source Sans 3 provides legible UI, Indonesian copy and factual metadata. Warm paper, deep green, restrained blue and a small gold rule support documentary imagery. Indexed open programme entries, dated activity rows, independent publication entries and a substantial field-learning band express different content meanings. No SaaS dashboard, booking cards, gradients, glass effects, invented terrain graphic or image-generating tool is used.

This is not a new edition of the historical Jurnal Ekspedisi or Atlas Tursina experiments. Those remain historical evidence. Visual distinction should come from genuine material, institutional meaning, hierarchy and disciplined component roles; a palette alone is not claimed to be unique to Tursina.
