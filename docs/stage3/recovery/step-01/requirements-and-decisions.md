## Proposed composition principles — no implementation

1. **Identity opening:** authorized identity, literal introduction, one documentary image and two useful actions. Copy precedes oversized media on mobile. One static image is the initial baseline; no carousel/video dependency.
2. **Breadth signposts:** compact routes into Tursina Explore, pendidikan/PD, other activities and works/knowledge. Existing routes stay canonical. Do not introduce period/angkatan as the universal hierarchy.
3. **Featured expedition:** TE11 can be the rich example, explicitly historical—not an unverified current announcement. A photo, short narrative and edition link introduce the full story without reproducing its whole log on Beranda.
4. **Education in practice:** GH navigation and RC teamwork establish learning through fieldwork, with divisi/angkatan metadata secondary to the readable activity title.
5. **Stories, publications and knowledge:** visibly separate independent works from activities; show a small authored selection and canonical return links.
6. **Institutional continuity:** a concise Jejak/Tentang entry gives the founding/history context and generational character. Buku Induk remains the simple synthetic catalogue during Stage 3.
7. **Current information only when verified:** no manufactured recruitment/event/date. If absent, the page should still feel complete through historical and institutional content.

This is a hierarchy to evaluate with references, not a pixel wireframe, fixed section count or approved final layout. Art direction must be chosen after image/copy review and desktop/mobile compositions.

## Component roles to evaluate in Step 2

| Role | Intended purpose | Consistency rule |
|---|---|---|
| Identity/header | Explain institution and provide public routes | Review controls must remain separate development chrome; approved IA retained |
| Institutional introduction | Literal identity, purpose and primary action | Not interchangeable with expedition/story title |
| Programme signpost | Identify recurring programme and its value | Same title/deck/action pattern for equivalent purposes |
| Activity preview | Date, place, lifecycle, depth and canonical link | Rich/basic variants share meaning; no forced media or empty sections |
| Documentary media | Cleared image, alt, caption, credit, crop and contextual source | Central media record; rights HOLD prevents public asset use |
| Publication preview | Format, authorship/date when verified, readable title and related activity | Do not duplicate expedition identity; no tourism price/rating metadata |
| Story section | Narrative, field evidence, gallery and learning | Deliberate expressive layouts; shared reading measure and heading roles |
| Evidence disclosure | Source confidence, material conflicts and publication limits | Secondary visual priority; never suppress important uncertainty |

No new component is built by this study. These roles should guide reuse or refinement after approval, with central token changes and cross-page verification.
