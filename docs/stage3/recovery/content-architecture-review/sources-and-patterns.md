# Part A — What institutional activities actually look like

## Evidence convention

**V** = directly examined source statement, not independent certification of the event. **I** = inferred recurring pattern across records. **R** = product recommendation. **O** = unresolved. A draft report or blank approval page is not a ratified master. Private custody locators are retained separately; safe references below identify document, section and, for PDFs, physical page. DOCX references use headings; printed page labels in the GH contents are not claimed as verified rendered pagination.

## What this evidence does NOT establish

No newly verified detailed BATAFIA session syllabus, technical achievements of PD29, automatic equivalence of Rally/Pengenalan across all years, institutional acceptance of every LPJ, current event announcement, or unrestricted publication right for source manuscripts. No original roster is a public content requirement. Personal health, absence/discipline reasons, individual progression, finances, private contacts and operational annexes are excluded from proposed public forms and examples.
